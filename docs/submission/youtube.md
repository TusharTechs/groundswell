Groundswell: AI agents rehearse your launch in a living model of the city (Qloo + Claude)

## Description

YouTube rejects angle brackets in descriptions; this text has none.

```
Every launch is a bet on where its audience is: a singer's first headline show, a comedy night, a pop-up, a film's preview week. Groundswell rehearses it first.

It builds a model of a real city from Qloo's taste graph, then a team of Claude agents rehearses your launch inside it hundreds of times: they find where the taste lives, pick real partner places, test plans in a simulator of word of mouth against a same-budget benchmark, and hand you the plan that worked. Then the Creative Director writes a launch kit for each neighbourhood, checking every cultural reference on Qloo first, and mission control steers the campaign from tracked QR scans while the Field Analyst moves the budget that's left.

Try it (no sign-in): https://groundswell-city.vercel.app
The Chicago rehearsal in this video: https://groundswell-city.vercel.app/rehearse/chicago-headline
How it works, with the evidence: https://groundswell-city.vercel.app/method
Code (MIT): https://github.com/TusharTechs/groundswell
Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/groundswell-a-flight-simulator-for-culture

What's real and what isn't
- The Chicago launch is fictional (Marisol Vega is invented); the city, venue, comparables and Qloo data are real.
- Reach figures are simulated, not forecasts. The mission-control week shown is simulated and labelled so in the app.
- From comparables alone, Groundswell found 44.8% of a taste's strongest neighbourhoods, against 23.7% for Claude alone (77 tests across 7 cities); method and controls are on the How it works page.
- The narration is an AI voice. The screen recording is the live app; captions, chapter labels and the end card were added, and the recorded rehearsal's replay runs slightly slower than normal to match the narration.

Built for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon with the Qloo Taste AI API, Anthropic Claude, Next.js, deck.gl and MapLibre.
```
