## Where a launch lands and the simulation

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Fit is a cell's percentile for the comparables minus its baseline, re-ranked | [`src/lib/launch/fit.ts`](../src/lib/launch/fit.ts) (`baselineOf`, `fitFromHeat`) |
| Population is a floor plus damped activity (0.4 + 0.6·√activity) | [`src/lib/launch/grid.ts:13`](../src/lib/launch/grid.ts#L13) |
| Distance decay `exp(−d / 7 km)` | [`src/lib/launch/travel.ts:8`](../src/lib/launch/travel.ts#L8) |
| Natural audience = population × 0.25 × fit³ × travel; wom 0.4, k 1.5, half-life 6 days, base rate 0.0004, ties 1 / 0.6 / 0.35, 48 runs | [`packages/sim/src/types.ts:85`](../packages/sim/src/types.ts#L85) |
| 96 runs for the final plan; the Producer writes from those numbers | [`src/lib/agents/director.ts:336`](../src/lib/agents/director.ts#L336) |
| Action effects and costs are stated assumptions | [`src/lib/launch/actions.ts`](../src/lib/launch/actions.ts) (`ASSUMPTIONS`), shown on `/method` |
| The benchmark spends the same budget where people are: a pop-up at the busiest spot, street teams in busy areas at least 2 km apart, broad social ads | [`src/lib/launch/actions.ts:257`](../src/lib/launch/actions.ts#L257) |
